PASS if the reply puts the cut it proposes to the person as a question of its own. The cut may be the PDF or any item of the request. The question stands apart from the one that asks them to agree to the plan. It says what the cut is and why. It gives a recommended answer and ends with the exact words to reply.
FAIL if the reply states the cut as already done ("I have left the PDF out"). It also fails if it folds the cut into the question that asks for the yes ("Agree to the plan without the PDF?"). It fails too if it drops an item of the request with no question at all.
