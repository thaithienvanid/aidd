# Domain packs

Every domain has a pack: a skill named `aidd-team:<pack>`, generated from `schemas/roles.json`, with the domain's standards and their editions, a checklist per loop step, and test commands to put in the Spec's tests. Load the packs for every domain the change touches, on top of your role's playbook; a pack's test commands are best practices the gate runs, not prose. No pack for the domain, or a standard checked more than a year ago? The loop contract's research-first rule applies: look up the authoritative source, record the edition in the Spec, and propose the pack or the update.
