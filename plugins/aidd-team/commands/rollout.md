Loop step: **Roll out**, on a Spec-first change and the emergency route. Start only when Merge approval holds and the change is merged. A deploy is `deploy.run`: ask the person first, and deploy only on their yes.

Lanes: spec, emergency. A change with no Spec has no observe window: its roll out is its merge and the project's usual deploy.

1. Run these subagents in parallel for $ARGUMENTS:
   - `aidd-team:sre`, for the report and for the rollout's waves and their state; a pipeline change is a new change
   - `aidd-team:compliance-analyst`, for the audit evidence map, when the Spec has compliance obligations

   The merged change's scope is closed. So the compliance analyst returns the map as obligation, control, and evidence rows in its return template (the aidd skill's `references/spec-returns.md`). These rows go into the rollout report. Writing the map under `docs/compliance/` is a documentation Task in a follow-up change whose scope covers that path.
2. The SRE owns the report:
   1. the observe window: the Spec's observe signals, on the project's dashboards and alerts, until the window ends or the trigger fires. It can run for hours, so watch it in the background. When the window ends after your session will, stop with `waiting` and the time it ends (`waiting: observe until <date and time>`)
   2. a comparison with the SLO and the rollback trigger
   3. a report to the author

   Agents report. The person decides the result, and the lead records it in the Spec's `outcome`.
3. If the trigger fires, say so first and stop. Ask the person whether to roll back, and roll back only on their yes. Record it, and the loop goes to Learn (`/aidd-team:postmortem`).
4. Optional, after a clean window: when the merged Spec's Design changed the system's shape, offer the author a follow-up change. The follow-up folds the Design into `docs/architecture/README.md`, the living architecture overview, as its own change through the loop. Open it only if the author agrees.
5. Once the change merged, list the worktrees and Task branches it left (`git worktree list`). Ask before you remove them (`git.branch.delete`).
