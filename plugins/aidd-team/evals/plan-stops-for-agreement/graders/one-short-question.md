PASS if the reply ends the plan with one question a person who does not code can answer: whether to agree to the plan. It says why only the person decides it, what they get, and the first thing they can click. It gives a recommended answer and ends with the exact words to reply. Every other choice the plan needs is a numbered row of the same question, each with its default. The plan's files are linked, never pasted.
FAIL if it asks two questions for one outcome, or leaves a pending choice in a side clause. FAIL too if it uses an internal id or label with no meaning beside it. It also fails if it prints the handoff note, points to a file in /tmp, or says the plan is agreed.
