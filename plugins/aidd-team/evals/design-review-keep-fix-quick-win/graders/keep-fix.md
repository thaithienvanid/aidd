PASS if the review sorts its points into Keep, Fix, and Quick win; the missing focus ring, the raw colour, and the unnamed button are Fix points; the steady loading state is a Keep; the bare empty state is a Quick win or a Fix with a reason; every point carries proof a reader can open or rerun (a screenshot path, the token, the file and line, the axe rule, a WCAG 2.2 success criterion such as 2.4.7 or 4.1.2, or a named Nielsen heuristic); and it declines the score out of 10, saying the list of Fix points is the verdict.
FAIL if the review gives a numeric score, grade, or percentage, leaves a point without proof, mixes the kinds into one undifferentiated list, or says the change is approved.
